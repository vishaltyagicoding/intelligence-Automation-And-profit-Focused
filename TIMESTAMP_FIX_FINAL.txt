**PIVOT TIMESTAMP vs CONFIRMATION TIMESTAMP**

HOW REVERSAL SIGNALS WORK:
1. At 13:15:00 - Price forms a PIVOT LOW (potential reversal point)
2. At 13:20:00 - Price moves up slightly
3. At 13:25:00 - Price continues up
4. At 13:30:00 - Price continues up
5. At 13:35:00 - Price continues up
6. At 13:40:00 - Price continues up
7. At 13:45:00 - Price continues up
8. At 13:50:00 - Price continues up
9. At 13:55:00 - SIGNAL CONFIRMED! (price moved far enough from pivot)

THE BUG:
- Signal is CONFIRMED at 13:55:00 (when it appears on chart)
- But code was using PIVOT timestamp (13:15:00) for the email
- Email shows "Signal generated at 13:15:00" (40 minutes old!)
- User receives email at 13:55:00 but thinks it's 40 minutes late

ACTUAL TIMELINE:
  13:15:00 - Pivot forms (NOT a signal yet)

  13:55:00 - Email sent INSTANTLY
  13:55:02 - User receives email

REALITY:

THE FIX
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CHANGED IN: trading/consumers.py (_emit_live_signal function)

BEFORE (WRONG):

```python
}

```

TECHNICAL EXPLANATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

REVERSAL SIGNAL DETECTION PROCESS:

1. PIVOT FORMATION (13:15:00):
   - Price makes a low point
   - This is stored as last_zone["timestamp"]
   - NOT a signal yet - just a potential reversal point

2. CONFIRMATION PERIOD (13:15:00 - 13:55:00):
   - Price moves away from pivot
   - Algorithm waits for sufficient movement
   - Checks multiple conditions (swing direction, threshold, etc.)

3. SIGNAL CONFIRMATION (13:55:00):
   - Price has moved far enough from pivot
   - All conditions met
   - Signal is CONFIRMED and appears on chart
   - This is df.index[-1] (current candle timestamp)

4. EMAIL SENDING (13:55:00):
   - Signal saved to database with CURRENT timestamp
   - Django signal handler triggers INSTANTLY
   - Email sent with CURRENT timestamp
   - User receives email within 1-2 seconds

OLD CODE PROBLEM:
- Used last_zone["timestamp"] (pivot time from step 1)
- Email showed 13:15:00 even though signal confirmed at 13:55:00
- Created illusion of 40-minute delay

NEW CODE SOLUTION:
- Uses df.index[-1] (confirmation time from step 3)
- Email shows 13:55:00 (actual confirmation time)
- Matches chart signal time EXACTLY
